How Elections Work

  • 1

    Announcement & Campaigning

    Candidates announce they’re running and begin connecting with voters.

  • 2

    Primaries & Caucuses

    Parties choose their official candidates through voting events.

  • 3

    National Conventions

    Parties formally nominate their candidates and unite their supporters.

  • 4

    General Campaign

    Candidates campaign nationwide to win over voters.

  • 5

    Election Day

    Citizens cast their votes to choose their leader.

  • 6

    Final Steps & Inauguration

    The winner is officially confirmed and sworn into office.

Announcement & Campaigning

This is where everything begins. Candidates announce their plans to run for office, build their teams, travel across regions, and share their ideas to gain public support.
